Product Hunt alternatives for indie founders

Launch days are crowded. Pinggy's 2026 guide counts 500+ products launching on Product Hunt every day, and most of them sink within hours. BACKD is one of the alternatives built around a lasting public verdict instead of a 24-hour spike.

List your startup free

What BACKD does differently

Founders submit a startup in about a minute. The community votes Back or Pass, and every startup gets a public page with its backing ratio, plus placement on the Explore, Trending and Rankings boards.

The page is also readable by machines. AI assistants can query BACKD through an open MCP server or a REST API and receive structured startup data instead of marketing copy.

Where it fits in a launch plan

Several 2026 launch guides make the same point: combining three or four platforms in one launch week beats betting on a single day. Use BACKD as the evergreen listing, and pair it with a pre-launch list such as BetaList and a community post such as Show HN or Indie Hackers.

Questions

What is a good Product Hunt alternative for bootstrapped startups?

BACKD is built for early-stage and bootstrapped startups. Listing is free, and ranking depends on community Back or Pass votes, not on audience size.

Can I use BACKD and Product Hunt together?

Yes. Many founders list on several platforms during launch week and keep one listing live for ongoing discovery.
