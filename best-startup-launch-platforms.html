Best startup launch platforms in 2026

No single platform is enough. The pattern across recent launch guides is to sequence several: pre-launch, launch day, then one evergreen listing.

List your startup free

The shortlist

  • BACKD: community Back or Pass verdicts, public rankings, and an MCP and REST catalog for AI agents. (This is our site, so weigh that accordingly.)
  • BetaList: pre-launch, for beta testers and waitlist signups.
  • Dev Hunt: developer tools, with a GitHub-oriented audience.
  • Peerlist Launchpad: launches inside a professional network.
  • Uneed, Fazier, MicroLaunch, PeerPush: smaller launch boards that are less crowded than Product Hunt.
  • Show HN and Indie Hackers: communities where a launch is a conversation.

How to sequence them

Start with a waitlist before you ship, launch on two or three boards in the same week, and keep one directory listing live afterwards. BACKD works well as that last step because the page keeps collecting votes and stays readable by AI agents.

Questions

What is the best place to launch a startup in 2026?

It depends on stage and audience. Use a pre-launch list, one or two launch boards, a community post, and a long-lived directory listing.

Are startup directories worth it?

They help most when you are early and have no audience, and when a listing stays live and discoverable after launch day.
