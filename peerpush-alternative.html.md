# A PeerPush alternative with community verdicts

> Canonical page: https://backd.io/peerpush-alternative.html

PeerPush and BACKD are both startup discovery platforms that care about being found by AI systems. They differ in how a startup earns its position.

[List your startup free](https://backd.io/auth/login)

## How BACKD works

Every listing receives public Back or Pass votes from the community. The result is a visible backing ratio and a place on leaderboards. A listing is a persistent public page, not only a launch-day event.

## Choose BACKD if

- You want an open community verdict on your startup, positive or not.

- You want agents to query your listing through MCP or REST with no API key.

- You are bootstrapped and want free listing.

## Choose PeerPush if

- You prefer its launch-board format and community. PeerPush describes its own product data as structured for AI recommendation, so review it side by side before deciding.

## Questions

### Is BACKD a PeerPush alternative?

They serve a similar purpose, startup discovery with AI visibility. BACKD's difference is the public Back or Pass community verdict and an open MCP server.

### Does BACKD guarantee AI assistants will recommend my startup?

No. BACKD makes your listing queryable and measures real reads from its own logs. It does not claim any model cited or recommended you.
